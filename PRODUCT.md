# Product

## Register

product

## Users

This site is for classmates, study partners, and friends who want to find the owner's learning materials, curated photos, scenic images, and reading notes. Most visitors arrive on mobile or a campus network, scan for useful resources first, and only read long posts when a resource looks relevant.

## Product Purpose

The blog is a dynamic resource discovery hub: it should make study files and photography collections easy to find, invite classmates to participate, and keep the owner's personal voice visible without hiding the practical resources. Success means users can quickly find a document, photo set, background image, or music atmosphere, then comment, contribute, or import new material into the blog flow.

## Brand Personality

Calm, field-minded, useful. The interface should feel like a quiet study camp with a practical resource desk: visually atmospheric, but task-first.

## Anti-references

Avoid a pure portfolio page where the owner profile dominates and resources are buried. Avoid decorative glass panels that make every surface look equally important. Avoid relying on overseas-only assets or third-party scripts for core content in a China-facing deployment.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the resource job: learning materials and photos should appear before long-form ambience.
2. Keep participation visible: classmates should see clear places to comment, submit resources, and react.
3. Atmosphere supports focus: motion, background images, and sound should help discovery, not distract from it.
4. Design for domestic deployment: external imagery, music, and crawler endpoints should be configurable and cacheable.
5. Make dynamic systems legible: crawler-like features need progress, source labels, and import actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Keep motion optional through reduced-motion CSS. Maintain keyboard-accessible buttons, visible focus states, and readable Chinese copy at mobile sizes.
